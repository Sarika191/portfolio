About Me

I’m Sarika Kaushal, a passionate Machine Learning trainee, full-stack developer, and creative tech enthusiast, currently pursuing B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) from Madan Mohan Malaviya University of Technology (MMMUT), in my final year.

I’ve worked as a Machine Learning Trainee & Intern at Krutanics (June–Sept 2025), where I built a Credit Card Fraud Detection system using Python, MySQL, and ML techniques, with certification jointly issued by Krutanics & Accenture. As a Full Stack Developer Intern at Unified Mentor (Mar–Apr 2025), I developed a student-teacher appointment system and my portfolio website using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Firebase. Earlier, as a Graphic & Content Intern at Inamigos Foundation (Oct–Nov 2024), I created digital content including reels, posters, and blogs for outreach campaigns.

Beyond internships, I actively build projects — from ML-powered fraud detection to interactive web apps and C++ games — all published on my GitHub. I also served as an Executive Member of Google Developer Student Clubs (GDG), MMMUT, leading hackathons, project expos, and orientations, sharpening my leadership skills.

With skills in Python, C++, Java, Firebase, Web Development, and Machine Learning, and achievements like LeetCode rating 1460, CodeChef 1148, and HackerRank 4★ in Java & Problem Solving, I aim to innovate at the intersection of technology and creativity.

Outside of tech, I love anime, manga, and painting — having received UNESCO recognition and the Amrita Sher-Gil Award for visual arts. These experiences inspire my imagination and fuel my creativity.

Let’s build, innovate, and create impact — one project at a time!
